About Me

Hello! I am Haoke Han (韩昊珂), an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Qingdao University (2023 - present). My research centers on trustworthy machine learning, with a focus on graph unlearning, privacy auditing, and the security of AI agents.

I am interested in how data removal and agent behaviors can leave hidden but auditable traces, and in building methods that make these systems verifiably safe. As first author, I have work accepted at ICANN 2026 and MDPI Information, with several papers under review at venues including IEEE TIFS and NDSS.
