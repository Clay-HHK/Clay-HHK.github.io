About Me

Hello! I am Haoke Han (韩昊珂), an undergraduate student in Computer Science and Technology at Qingdao University (2023 - present). My research spans three directions:

  • Data Mining & Privacy Protection: I study how to remove the influence of specific data from trained models, with a focus on graph unlearning in federated and long-tailed settings. A core thread is privacy auditing — using geometric and representation-level signals to verify that unlearning has actually taken effect, rather than taking it on faith.
  • LLM Agents & Agent Memory: I study how long-horizon coding agents learn from past failures through memory. My current work asks whether a reused memory will cause harm several steps later — not just immediately — and builds calibrated reliability estimates against these deferred consequences, measured by ECE and Brier score on real agent trajectories.
  • Continual Learning & Capability Preservation: I am interested in how models undergo capability collapse during continual learning — where acquiring new knowledge systematically degrades previously stable abilities. I focus particularly on small models, where limited capacity makes this tension between plasticity and stability more acute.

As first author, I have work accepted at ICANN 2026 and MDPI Information, with several papers under review at venues including IEEE TIFS and NDSS.

Projects

Built on a Cloudflare Worker serverless backend with peer-to-peer delivery, group broadcast, and shared-space sync, natively integrating MCP and Claude Code, and shipped via npm, Docker, and uv.
Company Agent: A multi-agent collaboration framework modeled after company organizational structures. It routes tasks across three tiers by complexity, decomposes complex tasks into a DAG for parallel execution, and supports multi-model configuration, hierarchical memory, and an experience-feedback loop with a React-based execution visualizer.
